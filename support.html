2. Getting Started

How do I create a schedule?

Open Timezone Agenda, select your project, choose your date range and cities, then tap Continue to view your schedule.

What is the Main City?

The Main City is the primary time zone used to organize the schedule timeline. Other selected cities are displayed in relation to the Main City.

3. Premium Upgrade

Is Premium a subscription?

No. Timezone Agenda Premium is a one-time In-App Purchase, not a recurring subscription.

What does Premium include?

Premium unlocks additional scheduling capabilities, including more cities, projects, events, extended date ranges, and PNG/PDF export options.

Current features and purchase pricing are shown within the App before purchase.

4. Restore Purchases

How can I restore Premium?

Open the Premium upgrade screen and select Restore Purchases.

Make sure you are using the Apple Account associated with your original purchase. An internet connection may be required to verify your purchase.

What if Restore Purchases does not work?

Check that you are signed in with the correct Apple Account and try again. If the issue continues, contact Timezone Agenda Support.

5. Projects and Events

Where is my scheduling information stored?

Projects, events, selected cities, and scheduling preferences are stored locally on your device.

Can I recover events after deleting the App?

Deleting the App and its local data may permanently remove your saved scheduling information. Timezone Agenda does not currently provide an account-based cloud synchronization service.

Restoring a Premium purchase does not automatically restore deleted projects or events.

6. Export and Share

Can I export my schedule?

Premium users can export schedules as PNG images or PDF documents.

How can I share an exported schedule?

Use the App's Export feature and select an available sharing option on your device.

7. Troubleshooting

Why do times differ between cities?

Timezone Agenda displays times using each city's time zone, including applicable daylight saving time rules.

Check your selected Main City, dates, and time zones if a displayed time is different from what you expect.

What should I do if the App is not working?

Try closing and reopening the App. If the issue continues, contact support with a description of the problem.

Avoid deleting the App as a troubleshooting step unless you understand that locally saved schedules may be lost.
