Timezone Agenda

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 7, 2026

Timezone Agenda ("the App") is a time zone scheduling application that helps users organize events and schedules across multiple cities and time zones.

We respect your privacy and are committed to explaining how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Timezone Agenda does not require account registration or sign-in.

Your scheduling information, including projects, events, selected cities, time zones, and preferences, is stored locally on your device.

We do not operate a server that receives or stores your scheduling information.

2. In-App Purchases

Timezone Agenda offers an optional, one-time Premium upgrade through Apple's In-App Purchase system.

Payments are processed by Apple. We do not directly collect or store your credit card information.

We use RevenueCat, a third-party purchase management service, to validate purchases, manage Premium access, and restore previous purchases.

RevenueCat may process purchase history, transaction information, an automatically generated anonymous App User ID, and related technical information necessary to provide its services.

Purchase-related information is also used for purchase analytics and maintaining Premium functionality.

For more information, see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy .

3. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Timezone Agenda does not integrate a separate advertising SDK or a dedicated third-party user behavior analytics service.

We do not use your scheduling information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

RevenueCat provides purchase-related analytics as part of its purchase management services.

4. Exporting and Sharing

The App allows users to export schedules as images or PDF files.

These files are generated on your device. If you choose to share an exported file through another application or service, the handling of that file is subject to the privacy practices of the service you select.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Purchase-related information is processed by Apple and RevenueCat to support purchases, purchase verification, Premium access, purchase restoration, and purchase analytics.

Your locally stored scheduling information is not transmitted to us.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your scheduling information is stored locally on your device. You can remove this information by deleting the relevant content within the App or by deleting the App and its local data from your device.

Timezone Agenda does not maintain a server that stores your scheduling information.

Purchase-related data is processed by Apple and RevenueCat in accordance with their respective data retention policies.

For requests to delete purchase-related data processed by RevenueCat on our behalf, please contact us at timezoneagenda.support@gmail.com .

For information about Apple's data retention and privacy practices, please visit Apple's Privacy Policy .

7. Children's Privacy

Timezone Agenda is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through account registration or user profiles.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App, its services, or applicable requirements.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
